About BEMBA

Okinawa's sea, at your own pace.

Concept

Our Concept

BEMBA is a private marine tour shop based in the Onna Village to Kin Town area of Okinawa, offering exclusive one-group-only tours.

"Yonna~, yuntaku." — Okinawan words for "take it easy and have a good chat." That spirit defines the BEMBA experience. We create an entirely private setting for your family, couple, or group of friends — so you can enjoy Okinawa's ocean at your own pace, without a care in the world.

Through a diverse lineup of tours — SUP, sea kayak, clear kayak, snorkeling, and drone photography — we put everything we have into sharing the beauty and joy of Okinawa's ocean with every guest.

Features

Why Choose BEMBA

01

One Group Only — Fully Private

Every tour is reserved for your group alone. No mixing with other guests — enjoy the ocean at your own pace.

02

Generous Time in the Water

SUP and kayak tours are 120 min in the water; snorkeling is 90 min; SUP + Snorkeling and Kayak + Snorkeling tours are 150 min. We never rush — the sea is meant to be enjoyed.

03

Photos & Video Included

All photos and video footage taken by the guide during your tour are included in the price. Keep the memories forever.

04

Family-Friendly Kids Pricing

For SUP, kayak, SUP + Snorkeling, and Kayak + Snorkeling tours, up to 3 children (18 and under) join free. Great for families.

05

Safety First

Our guide's background in firefighting, water rescue, and marine guiding ensures every tour is run with safety as the top priority.

06

Okinawa Resident Discount

Groups of 2+ adults with at least 1 Okinawa resident receive ¥2,000 off per adult.

Safety

Our Safety Commitment

"Safety before fun." — BEMBA applies the following safety practices on every tour.

Before Every Tour

  • Guide accompaniment throughout
  • Weather and sea condition check
  • Guest health check
  • Location change / cancellation judgment

Safety Equipment

  • Life jackets and safety gear
  • Rescue tube
  • Rescue rope
  • Guide kayak / SUP for rescue

Emergency Management

  • First aid kit on site
  • Waterproof mobile phone
  • Guest roster management
  • No participation under alcohol, drugs, or illness

Area

Tour Area

Tours are held primarily in the Onna Village and Kin Town area of Okinawa, with the exact location chosen based on sea conditions on the day. You will generally be notified of the meeting point the day before. If a last-minute decision is needed, we will contact you no later than 1 hour before the tour start time.

The exact meeting location is confirmed after booking. Please feel free to ask us via LINE if you have any questions.
