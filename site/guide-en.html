Meeting Point

Meeting Location & Tour Area

Onna Village & Kin Town area, Okinawa

The exact meeting point may change based on sea conditions on the day. We will generally notify you the day before, so there is no need to worry. If a last-minute decision is required, we will let you know no later than 1 hour before the tour starts.

Tours begin and end at the beach — there is no central shop. The detailed meeting point is confirmed after your booking is finalized.

What to Bring

What to Bring

Recommended items

  • A change of clothes
  • Towel
  • Sandals
  • Drinks / water
  • Wetsuits may be available for rental — please confirm via LINE in advance

What to wear

Please arrive in a swimsuit or rash guard. You will get wet during the tour.

Facilities

  • A portable changing tent and a simple rinse station (water tank) are available on site
  • There are no permanent changing rooms, showers, or toilets. Please use nearby public facilities before meeting us
  • Please ask us via LINE if you have any questions beforehand

Kids & Infants

Children & Infant Participation

Kids Pricing (Child = 18 and under / Adult = 19 and over)

  • SUP and kayak tours: up to 3 children free per booking. From the 4th child: ¥5,000 each
  • Snorkeling only: ¥5,000 per child from the 1st child
  • SUP + Snorkeling and Kayak + Snorkeling sets: ¥5,000 per child from the 1st child (snorkeling component fee; the SUP/kayak 3-child-free rule does not apply)
  • Clear kayak: when the 3-person exception (2 adults + 1 child age 5 or under) is approved, the child is free
  • Free children still count toward the maximum capacity and safety headcount

Infant participation (Sea Kayak)

  • Ages 1–under 2: guardian in same kayak required + guests must bring a properly fitting child life jacket of their own
  • Under 1: please consult us via LINE before booking
  • Regardless of age, participation is subject to individual safety assessment based on physique, health, equipment, and sea conditions on the day

Age Limit

Upper Age Limit

Water Activities (SUP, Kayak, Clear Kayak, Snorkeling & Combo Plans)

  • The standard upper age limit for water activities is 70 years old
  • Guests aged 71 or over are welcome to contact us via LINE before booking. The representative will assess individually whether safe participation is possible, taking into account the guest's health, physical condition, tour type, and sea conditions on the day
  • Prior consultation does not guarantee participation. Providing a headcount alone does not constitute approval for guests aged 71 or over

Beach & Drone Photography

  • No upper age limit

Cancellation

Tour Decisions & Cancellation Policy

How we decide whether to run the tour

  • Decisions are made the day before as a general rule
  • Rain alone does not cancel the tour — we operate in light rain
  • Typhoons, disasters, or sudden severe weather / sea conditions may result in cancellation
  • In unavoidable cases, the final decision may be made up to 1 hour before the start time

Cancellation fees

SituationFee
Guest cancellation the day before100% of tour price
Guest cancellation on the day100% of tour price (general rule)
Guest cancels due to BEMBA-proposed location / date changeNo cancellation fee
BEMBA cancels mid-tour due to weather / sea (under 30 min elapsed)Full refund
BEMBA cancels mid-tour (30 min or more elapsed)No refund

If your plans change, please let us know as soon as possible.
