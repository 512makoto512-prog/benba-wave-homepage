Why BEMBA

Why Guests Choose BEMBA

Five promises so you can relax and enjoy Okinawa's ocean to the fullest.

  • 01

    Fully private — one group only

    No sharing with other guests. Enjoy the tour at your own pace with just your family or friends.

  • 02

    Former firefighter as your guide

    Your guide has 16 years of firefighting service and water rescue unit experience, and reads the weather and sea carefully.

  • 03

    Up to 3 kids free (eligible tours)

    On SUP, Kayak, Mangrove SUP and Mangrove Kayak tours, up to 3 children (18 and under) per booking join free (¥5,000 each from the 4th child). Tours that include snorkeling are not eligible: ¥5,000 per child from the 1st child.

  • 04

    Free photos & videos

    All photos and videos taken by your guide are yours at no extra charge, so you can focus on the ocean.

  • 05

    Okinawa resident discount

    ¥2,000 off per adult when at least one Okinawa resident is in your group (conditions apply).
