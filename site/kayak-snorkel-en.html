Marine Tour

Kayak + Snorkeling

Experience approx. 150 min ／ Total approx. 210 min ／ Ages 5+ (under 5 by consultation) ／ Max 6

About Kayak + Snorkeling

Paddle by kayak,
then explore beneath the surface.

Kayak to the snorkeling spot, then jump in and explore the underwater world — two activities in a single outing.

BEMBA offers this as a private, one-group tour. The guide supports both activities and takes photos and videos throughout.

Max 6 guests total (children and free participants included). Minimum age: 5 (under 5 available on request).

Experience

What to Expect

01

Kayak to the Spot

Paddle your kayak across the water and reach the perfect snorkeling location.

02

Snorkel Beneath the Surface

After kayaking, jump in and explore coral reefs and tropical fish beneath the waves.

03

Photos & Videos Included

All photos and videos taken by your guide during the tour are yours to keep.

Info

Meeting Point, What to Bring & Notes

What to Bring

  • Change of clothes
  • Towel
  • Sandals
  • Water / drinks

Meeting Point

  • Meet and end at the beach (no shuttle)
  • Meeting location will be confirmed via LINE the day before
  • A portable changing tent and a simple rinse station (water tank) are available on site
  • There are no permanent changing rooms, showers, or toilets. Please use nearby public facilities before meeting us
  • Public restrooms nearby will be indicated

Safety Gear

  • Life jacket (provided)
  • Rescue tube
  • Rescue rope
  • Guide accompaniment for safety management

Notes

  • Please wear a swimsuit and rash guard
  • Max 6 guests total (children and free participants included)
  • Minimum age: 5 (under 5 available on request)
  • Location may change due to weather and sea conditions
Age Limit

Age Limit

  • This tour is open to participants up to age 70 as a general rule
  • Guests aged 71 or older who wish to join are asked to contact us via official LINE before booking. The representative will make an individual judgment based on the guest's health condition, fitness level, tour content, and sea conditions on the day
  • Prior consultation does not guarantee participation. Notifying us of your group size alone does not constitute approval to join
Cancellation

Tour Decisions & Cancellation

Go/No-Go Decision
We decide the day before, in principle. Rain alone does not cancel the tour.
Typhoons, disasters, or sudden severe weather/sea conditions may result in cancellation.
In unavoidable circumstances, a final decision may be made up to 1 hour before departure.

Guest cancellation (day before): 100% of tour fee

Guest cancellation (day of): 100% of tour fee in principle

BEMBA cancels mid-tour due to weather/sea conditions (under 30 min elapsed): Full refund

BEMBA cancels mid-tour due to weather/sea conditions (30 min or more elapsed): No refund

If BEMBA proposes a change in location or date and you cancel due to that change, no cancellation fee applies.

If your plans change, please let us know as early as possible.
Feel free to contact us on LINE with any questions.
