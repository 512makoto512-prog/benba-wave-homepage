Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Established: November 1, 2026

1. Types of Personal Information Collected

BEMBA ("we," "our," or "the shop") collects personal information from customers through the following means:

■ Via Google Form (booking application)

  • Full name
  • Contact information (phone number, email address, etc.)
  • Number of participants and age composition
  • Requested tour and preferred date

■ Via Official LINE account or email

  • Inquiry content and booking-related communications
  • Booking information equivalent to the above

■ Via paper participation form on the day of the tour

  • Full names and ages of all participants
  • Emergency contact information
  • Health status and declarations relevant to participation eligibility

For sensitive personal information such as health status, we obtain the participant's consent via the participation form or verbally before collecting such information.

2. Purpose of Use

Personal information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Accepting, confirming, modifying, and canceling bookings
  • Communicating tour details and meeting point information on the day of the tour
  • Responding to inquiries
  • Managing participant information for tour safety
  • Responding to emergencies
  • Complying with applicable laws and regulations

We do not use collected personal information for any other purpose, including marketing or sales promotion.

3. Security Measures

We manage customer personal information as follows:

■ Paper participation forms

  • Stored in locked files within our office
  • Accessible only by the representative

■ Electronic data (Google Form, LINE, etc.)

  • Managed using the security features provided by each service
  • Password management is maintained to prevent unauthorized access to our accounts

Server-side security for Google Form and LINE is governed by the respective service providers' policies.

4. Third-Party Disclosure and External Services

We do not provide personal information to third parties without the customer's consent, except in the following cases:

  • When required by law
  • When urgently necessary to protect a person's life, body, or property

■ External services used

We handle customer information through the following external services. Each service's own privacy policy applies to its use:

  • Google LLC (Google Forms): Used for booking applications. Submitted information is stored on Google's servers.
    → Google Privacy Policy
  • LINE Corporation (Official LINE Account): Used for booking and inquiry responses.
    → LINE Privacy Policy
  • Google LLC (Google Fonts): Used for website font display. IP addresses and similar information may be transmitted to Google when fonts are loaded.
    → Google Privacy Policy

5. Retention Period and Deletion

■ Paper participation forms

  • Retained for 1 year after the tour
  • Disposed of by shredding or equivalent method after the retention period

■ Google Form booking data (electronic)

  • Retained for approximately 1 year after the tour
  • Deleted from our Google Form account after the retention period

We cannot guarantee complete deletion of backup data retained by Google's systems.

■ LINE chat history

  • Retained on the LINE app as needed for booking and inquiry management

Retention and management on LINE's servers are subject to LINE's policies and are beyond our control.

■ Tour photos and videos

  • Data retained by BEMBA will be deleted approximately 90 days after delivery to the customer

We cannot guarantee complete deletion from cloud services or device backup areas, as these are subject to each service's specifications.

6. Photos and Video Data

Photos and videos taken by our staff during the tour are provided to customers as part of the tour fee.

■ Posting on SNS or our website

  • We post photos or videos on our SNS or website only when the customer has given explicit prior consent
  • Without consent, we will not publicly share any captured data
  • For minors, we also confirm consent from a parent or guardian

Consent is confirmed at the time of booking or on the day of the tour. If you wish to withdraw consent after giving it, please contact us at the inquiry address below.

7. Use of Cookies

Our website does not currently use its own cookies or access analytics tools such as Google Analytics.

However, when you use external links on our site (such as Google Forms, official LINE, or Google Maps), the cookie and data collection policies of those services apply.

8. Requests for Disclosure, Correction, Deletion, or Suspension of Use

Customers have the right to make the following requests regarding their personal information held by us:

  • Notification of purpose of use
  • Disclosure
  • Correction, addition, or deletion
  • Suspension of use or erasure
  • Suspension of third-party disclosure

Please contact us by email at the address below. We will verify your identity and respond within a reasonable timeframe.

For data held by external services such as Google Forms or LINE, our response will be limited to what is possible within those services' features and policies.

This policy may be revised due to changes in laws or our operating practices. Revised policies take effect upon posting on this page. We will notify users on our website when significant changes are made.
