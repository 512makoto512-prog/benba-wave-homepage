About SUP

Stand on the water.
Glide through Okinawa's ocean.

Stand-Up Paddleboarding (SUP) means standing on a wide board and paddling through the sea. In the calm, sheltered bays of Okinawa, it's easy to pick up even for first-timers. Because you're elevated above the waterline, the view of the crystal-clear water below is even more vivid.

Every BEMBA tour is fully private — just your group and your guide. Your guide will teach you everything from scratch, so there's nothing to worry about. Photos and video footage taken during the tour are included in the price, so you can focus entirely on enjoying the ocean.

Experience

What's included

01

Thorough land lesson

Before entering the water, your guide will walk you through balance and paddling on land. Perfect for complete beginners.

02

Paddle Okinawa's clear water

About 120 minutes gliding over transparent water at your own pace. Look down and watch the ocean floor pass beneath you.

03

Photos & video included

Your guide captures photos and video throughout the tour. All files are sent to you — no extra charge.

Info

What to bring & good to know

What to bring

  • Change of clothes
  • Towel
  • Sandals
  • Water / drinks
  • Wetsuit rental available on request

Meeting point

  • Meet and part ways directly at the beach
  • Exact location sent via LINE the day before
  • If conditions require a last-minute change, you'll be notified at least 1 hour before the tour
  • A portable changing tent and a simple rinse station (water tank) are available on site
  • There are no permanent changing rooms, showers, or toilets. Please use nearby public facilities before meeting us
  • Nearest public restroom pointed out on the day

Safety equipment

  • Life jacket (provided)
  • Rescue tube
  • Rescue rope
  • Guide accompanies the group throughout

Notes

  • Please arrive in swimwear or a rash guard
  • Minimum age 10 (under 10 available on request)
  • Maximum group size: 5
  • Location may change depending on sea and weather conditions
Age Limit

Age Limit

  • This tour is open to participants up to age 70 as a general rule
  • Guests aged 71 or older who wish to join are asked to contact us via official LINE before booking. The representative will make an individual judgment based on the guest's health condition, fitness level, tour content, and sea conditions on the day
  • Prior consultation does not guarantee participation. Notifying us of your group size alone does not constitute approval to join
Cancellation

Cancellation & weather policy

Go / No-go decision
Decision is made the day before, as a rule. Tours run in light rain. In case of typhoon, disaster, or sudden severe conditions, the tour may be cancelled.
In unavoidable circumstances, a final decision may be made up to 1 hour before the tour starts.

Cancellation by guest — day before: 100% of the tour fee

Cancellation by guest — same day: 100% of the tour fee (as a rule)

Tour cancelled mid-activity (under 30 min elapsed): Full refund

Tour cancelled mid-activity (30 min or more elapsed): No refund

If BEMBA proposes a change of location or date and you choose to cancel as a result, no cancellation fee applies.

If your plans change, please let us know as soon as possible.
Feel free to contact us on LINE for any questions.
